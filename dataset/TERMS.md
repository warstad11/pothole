# Data Use Terms

Plain-language terms for the Real-World Pothole Drives dataset. Not legal
advice — if you publish or build on this data, have an adult review these
terms first.

## You may

- Use the sensor data, labels, and blurred clips for **research and
  education**.
- Train and evaluate models, and publish results, with attribution to the
  Pothole Detection Lab project.

## You may not

- **Try to identify** any person, vehicle, license plate, home, business,
  or exact location shown or implied in this data.
- Combine this data with other data to undo the anonymization.
- Use it for any **commercial** purpose without permission.
- Re-publish the clips in a form that re-introduces removed information
  (e.g., adding GPS back, or de-blurring).

## Privacy and residual risk

The sensor data has had GPS, timestamps, device IDs, and street names
removed. The video clips have been auto-blurred for faces, license plates,
and text, and drive start/end segments were trimmed. **Blurring is
best-effort and imperfect** — distant signage and recognizable scenery may
remain. By using this data you acknowledge that residual re-identification
risk exists and you agree not to act on it.

## Reporting a problem

If you spot personal information that should have been removed (a clear
face, a readable plate, an identifiable location), please open an issue on
the project repository so it can be fixed or the clip removed.

## Recording consent

These recordings were made from a vehicle on public roads. The people and
vehicles incidentally captured did not give consent, which is why faces and
plates are blurred and why the re-identification ban above exists. Treat
the people in this data with the respect you would want for yourself.
